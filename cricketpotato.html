Bowled Over

Cricket names hot-potato charades
Setup: Sit in a circle, teams interleaved (T1, T2, T1, T2), so partners sit across from each other.
  1. One player holds the phone. They see a cricket player's name and give verbal clues — no saying the name, no gestures needed (charades-style is fine too).
  2. Their teammate sitting across guesses.
  3. Got it? The phone passes to the player on the other team next in the circle, who becomes the new clue-giver. Back and forth it goes.
  4. A hidden timer ticks — faster and faster. Nobody knows when it goes off.
  5. When the buzzer hits, whoever is holding the phone loses the round. The other team scores a point.
  6. First to the target score wins the match!
